The India Gate (originally the All India War Memorial) was designed by Sir Edwin Lutyens. The foundation stone was laid on 10 February 1921, and the memorial was unveiled on 12 February 1931.

It stands 42 metres tall and honours the 70,000 soldiers of the British Indian Army who died between 1914 and 1921 in the First World War and Third Anglo-Afghan War.
