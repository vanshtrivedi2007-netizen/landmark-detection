Hawa Mahal, or the 'Palace of Winds', was built in 1799 by Maharaja Sawai Pratap Singh and designed by Lal Chand Ustad.

The five-storey pyramidal facade rises 15 metres from its high base, resembling the crown of Krishna. Built of red and pink sandstone, its 953 casements or jharokhas were built to allow purdah-observing royal women to watch street life and processions unnoticed.
