Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 2019, the fortified historic city of Jaipur exhibits exceptional town planning integrating ancient Hindu grid concepts with Western design principles.

Hawa Mahal sits at the edge of the City Palace complex, echoing the distinctive pink terracotta wash mandated for the entire walled city.
