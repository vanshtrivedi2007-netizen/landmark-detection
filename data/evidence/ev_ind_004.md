The monument grounds and surrounding lawns are open 24 hours a day with free public access. Floodlights illuminate the arch every evening from 7:00 PM to 9:30 PM.

Pedestrian underpasses and designated food vendor zones ensure safe pedestrian flow around the bustling C-Hexagon roundabout.
