India Gate is situated at the eastern terminus of Kartavya Path (formerly Rajpath), directly aligned with the Rashtrapati Bhavan (Presidential Palace) to the west.

It serves as the focal point of the annual Republic Day parade held every 26 January, celebrating the enactment of India's constitution.
