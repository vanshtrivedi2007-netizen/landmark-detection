Delhi features an overlapping monsoon-influenced humid subtropical climate and semi-arid conditions. Summer lasts from early April to late June with heatwaves reaching 46 °C.

Winter months (December and January) experience heavy morning fog and cold spells, with low temperatures dropping to 4–7 °C.
