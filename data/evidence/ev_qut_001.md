Built in the early 13th century a few kilometres south of Delhi, the red sandstone tower of Qutb Minar tapers from a 14.32 m diameter base to 2.75 m at its peak, reaching 72.5 metres in height.

Construction was initiated by Qutb ud-Din Aibak in 1192 and carried forward by Shams-ud-din Iltutmish and later repaired and augmented by Firoz Shah Tughlaq.
