Designated a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2007, the Red Fort is a premier national monument. Each year on 15 August, India's Independence Day, the Prime Minister hoists the national flag at the Lahori Gate ramparts.

The fort's design represents the zenith of Mughal creativity under Shah Jahan, integrating Persian palace architecture with indigenous Indian traditions.
