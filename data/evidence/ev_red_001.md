The Red Fort Complex was built as the palace fort of Shahjahanabad, the fifth Mughal capital in Delhi, founded by Emperor Shah Jahan in 1638. Construction commenced in 1638 and was completed in 1648.

The fortress features massive red sandstone walls that rise up to 33 metres on the city side and 18 metres along the river.
