Four slender minarets stand at each of the four corners of the square plinth, each over 40 metres tall. The minarets are deliberately angled slightly outward to protect the central dome in case of collapse.

The exterior decoration features pietre dure inlay work using semi-precious stones embedded in translucent white Makrana marble.
