Inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 1993, the complex includes the celebrated 4th-century Gupta Iron Pillar, which possesses remarkable corrosion resistance.

The surrounding ruins also preserve the Quwwat-ul-Islam Mosque, notable as one of the oldest surviving mosques built on the Indian subcontinent.
