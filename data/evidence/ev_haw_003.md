The building is celebrated for its natural passive cooling mechanism. Small openings and lattices accelerate air currents through the Venturi effect, maintaining comfortable temperatures even during scorching desert summers.

The building lacks solid foundations in conventional terms and leans at an angle of 8 inches, stabilized by its curved pyramidal geometry.
